# Gesture-Based Web Instrument - Vision

**A musical instrument you play with your hand in the air, using only a web browser and a webcam, with a simple game that teaches you melodies.**

## Problem

Learning a traditional instrument takes time, money and physical skill before it becomes fun. Many people who like music never get to play a melody.

## Who it is for

- **Maria, 24, curious beginner (main persona):** wants to play something nice on her laptop within a minute, without signing up.
- **Tiago, 19, music student:** uses the game as ear training, on his laptop and his phone.
- **Ana, 45, limited range of motion (future persona):** needs the instrument to adapt to small movements. A calibration wizard after the MVP is mainly for her.

## Main features

- Your index fingertip is a pointer over **8 cells**, the notes of a scale. Opening your hand makes the note louder.
- The notes always belong to the chosen scale, so they always sound in tune.
- Two layouts: a **2×4 pad grid** and a **piano**.
- **Listen and Repeat** game: hear a short melody, play it back, get 1 to 3 stars.
- Nothing to install, no account needed. The camera video never leaves the device.
