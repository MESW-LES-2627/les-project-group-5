# Gesture-Based Web Instrument - Vision

**Learn, play and jam, with an accessible instrument that fits your movement.**

The Gesture-based Web Instrument is a browser-based musical instrument played with body gestures instead of other controllers. The user's webcam is analysed in real time with MediaPipe, and head or hand movements control the pitch, volume and other paraeters of a note. The base product is a main page with a grid of cells played through MediaPipe gestures, plus a settings page and a calibration page. On top of that base we propose extra features: learning exercises and network jam rooms.


## Product Vision Statement

**For** people who want to learn music and play with others
**Who** can’t get, or can’t play, a common physical instrument,
**The** Gesture-based Web Instrument **is** a web-based musical instrument for learning and playing.
**That** adapts to each person’s movement, so they can learn step by step and play online, using only a browser and a webcam.
**Unlike** mouse, touch or keyboard-driven virtual instruments, which assume fine hand control,
**Our product** uses gestures defined by the individual, so even a small movement reaches every note.

## Problem

Learning a traditional instrument takes time, money and physical skill before it becomes fun. Many people who like music never get to play a melody.

## Who it is for

- **Maria, curious beginner:** wants to learn and play something musical on her laptop within a minute, without having to buy external controllers or installing new software.
- **Tiago, music student:** wants to jam or play, doesn't have an instrument around but has a laptop or phone.
- **Ana, limited range of motion:** wants to learn music and needs the instrument to adapt to the movements she can do.

## Main features (in order of relevance)

| **Features**    | **Definition**                                           | **Importance (for MVP)**              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Platforms       | Web app for desktop and mobile browsers                  | High                                  |
| Privacy         | Nothing to install. Camera feed never leaves the device. | High                                  |
| Pitch control   | Head position (yaw, pitch) or hand position on screen    | High                                  |
| Volume control  | Mouth opening or hand opening                            | High                                  |
| Note layouts    | 8 grid cells or piano layout                             | High                                  |
| Learning game   | Hear a short melody, play it back, get 1 to 3 stars.     | Mid                                   |
| Vibrato control | Head roll or hand roll                                   | Low                                   |
| Jam rooms       | Network jam rooms for several players                    | Low                                   |
